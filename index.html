How to Play

1
A cat appears with a number on its back
2
Type the ordinal form in small letters
3
Press Enter or Go before the timer runs out
4
3 correct answers in a row give you bonus points
5
A mistake costs 1 life. You have 3 lives
6
Catch all 20 cats to win the game
7
Examples: 5 → fifth 12 → twelfth 21 → twenty-first
